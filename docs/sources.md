# Merapi Slope Village — fact sources (checked 12 Oct 2026)

Facts used on the nameplate / caption:

1. Merapi is an active stratovolcano on the border of Central Java and the Special Region of Yogyakarta, ~28 km north of Yogyakarta city; described as Indonesia's most active volcano; name means "Mountain of Fire".
   - Wikipedia, Mount Merapi: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merapi_volcano
   - Britannica: https://www.britannica.com/place/Mount-Merapi ("most active of Indonesia's 130 active volcanoes", "Mountain of Fire")
   - VolcanoDiscovery: https://www.volcanodiscovery.com/merapi.html ("Indonesia's most active volcano")
2. Coordinates 7.54°S, 110.446°E; elevation 2,910 m per Smithsonian GVP (other sources give 2,968 m, so the nameplate uses the GVP coordinates only, not a height).
   - Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program, Merapi (263250): https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=263250
3. Activity is characterised by growth and collapse of the summit lava dome producing pyroclastic flows, plus lahars (GVP profile above).
4. "Wedhus gembel" (Javanese: shaggy-wool sheep/goat) is the local name for Merapi's pyroclastic clouds/flows, because they roll in lumpy whitish billows like shaggy wool. These flows follow river valleys.
   - Kementerian ESDM: https://www.esdm.go.id/en/media-center/news-archives/bahaya-itu-bernama-wedhus-gembel
   - Kompas: https://www.kompas.com/stori/read/2022/12/04/170754579/wedhus-gembel-kambing-paling-tenar-tatkala-gunung-meletus
5. Rain-triggered lahars: 240+ lahars in the 2010–2011 rainy season after the 2010 eruption, down rivers under the cone (e.g. Boyong, Kuning, Gendol); deposits become sand/gravel used as building material (sand mining in the Gendol River).
   - de Bélizal et al. 2013, JVGR 261: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0377027313000280
   - Malawani et al., Forum Geografi (Gendol sand mining): https://journals.ums.ac.id/fg/article/view/8766

Artistic licence (not fact claims): village layout, house count, river/bridge placement, eruption timing in the toy, lahar speed.
6. Indonesian volcano alert levels used on the in-app status chip (labelled "simulasi"): Level I Normal, Level II Waspada, Level III Siaga, Level IV Awas (Permen ESDM No. 15/2011).
   - MAGMA Indonesia (PVMBG): https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/gunung-api/tingkat-aktivitas
   - BPBD DIY: https://bpbd.jogjaprov.go.id/berita/mengenal-tingkatan-status-gunung-api (notes the real Merapi has been Level III Siaga since 5 Nov 2020, so the toy's "Normal" start is a simulation state, not the real status)
